# Code of Conduct

Phoenix Net Generator welcomes technical discussion, bug reports, experiments, and contributions from people with different levels of EDA experience.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally
- critique technical ideas rather than individuals
- provide reproducible evidence when reporting bugs
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- respect intellectual property and confidentiality
- avoid posting proprietary schematics, libraries, credentials, or customer data without permission

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For sensitive security matters, follow the process in `SECURITY.md` rather than opening a public issue.
